# Two-reviewer process for the 25 isomorphic variants

All 25 variants completed this process before any variant was administered to a target model.

## Procedure

1. **Construction record.** The authors recorded each variant's surface changes, correct reasoning chain, necessary assumptions, and distractor correspondence.
2. **Reviewer 1 — independent blind solve.** Reviewer 1 solved each variant without access to the answer key and confirmed that it had one unique defensible answer.
3. **Reviewer 2 — equivalence review.** Reviewer 2 assessed construct equivalence, answer and distractor mapping, the absence of new confounds, self-containment, and approximate difficulty equivalence using G1–G5.
4. **Correction and re-check.** Required wording or figure corrections were completed and re-checked before approval. The constrained option plan was checked without disclosing the original–variant mapping to target models.
5. **Approval gate.** A variant could be deployed only after both reviews were complete, all G1–G5 criteria passed, and any disagreement had been adjudicated.

## Outcome

All 25 variants completed both reviews before deployment. Required corrections were completed and re-checked. Every variant received `approved` adjudication status and an `approved_for_deployment` final decision; row-level outcomes are provided in `two_reviewer_approval_outcomes.csv`.
