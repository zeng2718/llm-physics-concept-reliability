# Variant validity criteria (G1–G5)

These five validity gates were prespecified; a variant failing any one of them was not eligible for deployment.

An acceptable isomorphic variant must satisfy two requirements: **deep equivalence** (the variant measures the same physical concept, invokes the same solution path, and preserves the same misconception-diagnostic function as the source item) and **surface separation** (the answer cannot be retrieved directly from the source item's wording, answer letter, names, or figure appearance). Deep equivalence takes priority over formal similarity; surface separation takes priority over mechanical word-count control.

## The five gates

- **G1 — Construct identity.** The tested concept, physical law(s), necessary assumptions, and the shortest correct reasoning chain are identical between source item and variant.
- **G2 — Unique and semantically equivalent answer.** The variant must be solved independently by a reviewer; the correct option must be unique and must express the same physical judgment as the source item's correct option.
- **G3 — One-to-one distractor mapping.** Every source-item distractor has a semantically corresponding variant distractor diagnosing the same error rule, false assumption, or misconception. The mapping is recorded by option semantics, not by letter position.
- **G4 — No new confounds.** Material properties, altitude, friction, air resistance, time scale, device operation, or any other added factor must not additionally influence the answer, and no new plausible interpretation may be introduced.
- **G5 — Self-contained with approximately equivalent difficulty.** The variant is answerable without the source item; it must not add terms, observations, or qualifiers that directly cue the answer, and must not omit information required by the source item's solution.

## Surface-perturbation rules (summary)

- At least two surface dimensions were changed in principle (objects/actors, setting, values/units, figure appearance, or spatial orientation); name-only or synonym-only changes were not acceptable.
- Numerical items required changed values, with all options recomputed so that the correct option and each distractor's underlying error algorithm stayed aligned.
- Figure items were redrawn or meaningfully mirrored, preserving motion directions, relative positions, vector relationships, physical symmetry, and comparable information load.
- Transfer distance was controlled to near transfer: surface changes had to cut direct recall without changing the causal model of the problem.

Reading load, option length/style balance, and figure information function were recorded as descriptive QC rather than fixed thresholds.
